# Attribution

The speech in `conversations/` is built from clips of Multilingual LibriSpeech (Pratap et al., 2020), derived from LibriVox public-domain recordings, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/multilingual_librispeech.

Modifications: utterances were concatenated into two-speaker conversations, mixed with a fixed seed, normalized and re-encoded with the channel profiles listed in `MANIFEST.json`.
